# Design Patent Strategy

## Purpose

This document defines the strategy for obtaining design patent protection for the visual and ornamental aspects of the ADVIS platform hardware. Design patents protect the appearance (not function) of manufactured articles and provide 15 years of protection from grant date (US).

---

## Design Patent Candidates

### DP-001: Windshield-Mount Module Form Factor

**Article of Manufacture**: Compact dual-facing camera module for vehicle windshield mounting

**Ornamental Features to Protect**:
- Overall housing shape and proportions (elongated ovoid / aerodynamic profile)
- Dual opposing lens aperture arrangement (forward + cabin)
- Surface texturing and finish transitions between optical and non-optical surfaces
- Mounting surface profile and adhesive pad recess geometry
- LED indicator positioning and window shape
- Connector recess and cable exit profile

**Drawings Required**:
- 7 standard views: front, rear, top, bottom, left side, right side, perspective
- Environmental view showing module mounted on windshield (broken lines for vehicle)
- Detail views of lens aperture surrounds

**Filing Notes**:
- File before any product images are shown publicly (trade shows, OEM presentations)
- Consider multiple design patents for variations (with/without sun shield, different lens sizes)
- Use broken lines for functional elements not being claimed ornamentally

### DP-002: Modular SoM Blade Connector System

**Article of Manufacture**: System-on-Module blade connector for automotive computing platform

**Ornamental Features to Protect**:
- SoM blade edge profile and contact pattern visual arrangement
- Carrier-side receptacle housing shape and latch mechanism appearance
- Alignment guide rail visual profile
- Color/material contrast pattern between blade body and connector edge

**Drawings Required**:
- 7 standard views of SoM blade alone
- 7 standard views of carrier receptacle alone
- Mated assembly perspective view
- Exploded perspective showing insertion relationship

**Filing Notes**:
- Critical to file before SoM connector visible to any third-party SoM vendor
- Consider separate applications for: blade alone, receptacle alone, mated combination
- Include alignment features as part of ornamental design

### DP-003: Single-Cable Vehicle Integration Connector

**Article of Manufacture**: Hybrid automotive connector combining power, data, and coaxial channels

**Ornamental Features to Protect**:
- Connector body external shape and sealing surface profile
- Latch/lock mechanism visible appearance
- Keying feature external geometry (anti-mismating visual design)
- Cable exit boot shape and strain relief profile
- Color coding or marking pattern arrangement

**Drawings Required**:
- 7 standard views of plug (vehicle harness side)
- 7 standard views of receptacle (ECU housing side)
- Mated perspective view
- Detail view of latch mechanism visible elements

**Filing Notes**:
- Coordinate with connector supplier (Amphenol/TE/Molex) for co-ownership if applicable
- File before connector shown to any OEM customer
- May qualify for international design registration (Hague System)

## Design Patent vs. Trade Dress

| Protection | Scope | Duration | Registration |
|-----------|-------|----------|--------------|
| Design patent | Ornamental appearance of article | 15 years (US) | Required (filing + examination) |
| Trade dress | Product appearance as source identifier | Indefinite (if maintained) | Optional (but recommended) |

**Recommendation**: Pursue design patents for all three candidates. Consider trade dress registration for DP-001 (module form factor) once the product achieves market recognition.

## Drawing Standards

### US Design Patent Drawing Requirements

- Black and white line drawings (or formal photographs)
- All views must be consistent in scale and style
- Broken lines indicate environment/unclaimed elements
- Surface shading indicates 3D form and curvature
- No dimensions or measurements in drawings
- Reference numerals optional for design patents

### International Considerations

- Hague System: Single application covers multiple countries
- EU: Registered Community Design (unexamined, fast)
- China: Design patent (examined, 10-year term being extended to 15)
- Japan: Design registration (examined)
- India: Design registration (10 years, renewable to 15)

### Differentiation Strategy

The ADVIS module design is differentiated by:
1. Dual-facing optical paths (no competitor has this in windshield-mount form factor)
2. Compact ovoid profile optimized for mirror-shadow mounting
3. Integrated IR illumination window facing cabin
4. Single connector exit (vs. multi-cable competitors)
