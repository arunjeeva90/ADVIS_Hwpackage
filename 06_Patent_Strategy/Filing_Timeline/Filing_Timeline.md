## Key Deadlines and Triggers

### Absolute Deadlines (Cannot Be Extended)

| Deadline | Consequence of Missing | Affected |
|----------|----------------------|----------|
| 12 months from provisional filing | Lose priority date; must refile | All 5 provisionals |
| 30 months from priority date | National phase entry deadline (most countries) | PCT national entries |
| 12 months from non-provisional | Last date for PCT if not already filed | All non-provisionals |

### Business-Triggered Deadlines

| Trigger | Required Action | Lead Time |
|---------|----------------|-----------|
| Any public disclosure of ADVIS concept | Must have provisional filed BEFORE disclosure | 0 days (pre-file required) |
| OEM customer presentation with technical details | Confirm NDA in place OR patent filed | 2 weeks |
| Trade show / conference attendance | Review what will be disclosed; ensure patent coverage | 4 weeks |
| Product launch announcement | All relevant provisionals must be filed | 8 weeks |
| First product shipment to customer | Full non-provisional coverage recommended | 12 weeks |
