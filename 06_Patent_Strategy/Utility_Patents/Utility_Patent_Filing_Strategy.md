## Filing Strategy by Disclosure

### ID-003: Risk-Coupled Decision Fusion (Priority 1)

**Rationale for #1 Priority**: Most novel concept with strongest blocking potential. No existing system couples driver attention with scene risk multiplicatively. First-to-file establishes foundational claim position.

**Filing Plan**:
1. Provisional application (US): Month 1-2
2. Prior art search completion: Month 3-4
3. Non-provisional application (US): Month 10-11 (before 12-month deadline)
4. PCT international application: Month 12 (claiming priority to provisional)
5. National phase entries: Month 30 (from earliest priority date)

**Continuation Strategy**:
- CIP (Continuation-in-Part): Add learned coupling function embodiment after R&D data available
- Divisional: Separate claims for DMS-only risk assessment vs. coupled assessment
- Continuation: Broader/narrower claim scope based on prosecution history

### ID-001: SoC-Adaptive Platform (Priority 2)

**Rationale**: Core platform architecture patent. Protects the physical hardware innovation enabling multi-SoC flexibility.

**Filing Plan**:
1. Provisional application (US): Month 2-3
2. Prior art search completion: Month 4-5
3. Non-provisional application (US): Month 12-13
4. PCT international application: Month 14
5. National phase entries: Month 32

**Continuation Strategy**:
- Divisional: Separate claims for power sequencing alone vs. full adaptive platform
- CIP: Add embodiments as new SoC variants are validated on the platform
- Continuation: Platform-level claims vs. component-level claims

**Rationale**: Physical/thermal innovation highly relevant to product differentiation. Visible to competitors once product ships, so early filing critical.

## Provisional vs. Non-Provisional Timing

### Provisional Application Benefits

- Establishes priority date at lower cost ($1,600-3,000 filing fee)
- 12-month window to refine claims and conduct prior art search
- "Patent Pending" status immediately available
- No formal claims required (but recommended to include draft claims)

### Provisional Filing Requirements

Each provisional must contain:
- Complete written description of the invention
- At least one drawing/figure
- Cover sheet with inventor information
- Filing fee

### Non-Provisional Conversion Requirements

Within 12 months of provisional filing:
- Formal claims (reviewed by attorney)
- Complete specification with detailed description
- Professional patent drawings
- All formalities (oath/declaration, assignment)

## International Filing Strategy

### PCT (Patent Cooperation Treaty) Path

```
Provisional (US)     Non-Provisional (US)     PCT Application
  Month 0      -->     Month 10-12       -->    Month 12-14
                                                     |
                                            International Search
                                            Report (Month 16-18)
                                                     |
                                            National Phase Entry
                                            (Month 30-31)
```

### Priority Territories

| Territory | Rationale | Estimated Cost (per application) |
|-----------|-----------|----------------------------------|
| United States | Largest ADAS market, strong patent enforcement | $15,000-25,000 |
| European Patent Office (EP) | Major automotive OEM headquarters | $20,000-30,000 |
| China (CN) | Largest vehicle production market | $10,000-15,000 |
| Japan (JP) | Major automotive technology center | $12,000-18,000 |
| South Korea (KR) | Hyundai/Kia, Samsung automotive | $8,000-12,000 |
| India (IN) | Growing automotive market, local R&D presence | $5,000-8,000 |

### Territory Selection Criteria

File in a territory if ANY of the following apply:
1. Major automotive OEM headquarters located there
2. Significant vehicle production volume
3. Key competitor R&D center located there
4. Potential licensee operates there
5. Manufacturing of ADVIS modules planned there

## Risk Management

### Filing Risks

| Risk | Probability | Mitigation |
|------|------------|------------|
| Prior art invalidates claims | Medium | Early prior art search; broad + narrow claims |
| Competitor files first | Low | Aggressive provisional filing timeline |
| Budget constraints delay filing | Medium | Priority ranking; defer lower-priority filings |
| International filing costs exceed budget | Medium | Prioritize US + EP + CN; defer other territories |
| Publication before filing | Low | Strict pre-filing disclosure controls |

### Portfolio Monitoring

- Monthly status review of all applications
- Quarterly competitive filing review (monitor competitor patent applications)
- Annual portfolio valuation assessment
- Immediate response plan for potential infringement discoveries
