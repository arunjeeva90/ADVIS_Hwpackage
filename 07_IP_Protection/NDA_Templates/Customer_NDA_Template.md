## CUSTOMER NON-DISCLOSURE AND EVALUATION AGREEMENT

**This Agreement** is entered into as of [Date] ("Effective Date") by and between:

**[COMPANY NAME]** ("Discloser"), with principal offices at [address]

and

**[CUSTOMER/OEM NAME]** ("Recipient"), a [jurisdiction] entity with principal offices at [address]

---

## 1. Purpose

Discloser wishes to disclose to Recipient certain confidential information regarding the ADVIS (Adaptive Driver & Vehicle Intelligence System) platform for the purpose of Recipient's evaluation of a potential business relationship including, but not limited to: technology assessment, integration feasibility analysis, product planning, and commercial terms discussion (the "Evaluation Purpose").

---

## 2. Confidential Information

### 2.1 Definition

"Confidential Information" includes all non-public information disclosed by Discloser, including:

(a) Product specifications, architecture documents, and technical datasheets
(b) Performance data, benchmarks, and test results
(c) Roadmap information and future product plans
(d) Pricing, licensing terms, and commercial proposals
(e) Prototype hardware and evaluation samples
(f) Software, firmware, and API documentation
(g) Patent strategy information shared for evaluation

### 2.2 Exclusions

Standard exclusions apply (public knowledge, prior possession, independent development, third-party disclosure without restriction, legal compulsion).

### 2.3 Trade Secret Designation

Information specifically marked as "TRADE SECRET" or "RESTRICTED" is subject to enhanced protection obligations as described in Section 5.

---

## 3. Evaluation Terms

### 3.1 Evaluation Period

The evaluation period begins on the Effective Date and continues for [6/12 months] unless extended by mutual written agreement.

### 3.2 Permitted Evaluation Activities

Recipient may:
(a) Review technical documentation for integration feasibility assessment
(b) Evaluate prototype hardware in Recipient's labs (if samples provided)
(c) Conduct internal technical presentations to Recipient's evaluation team
(d) Perform compatibility testing with Recipient's vehicle systems

### 3.3 Restrictions During Evaluation

Recipient shall NOT:
(a) Benchmark Confidential Information against competitor products for publication
(b) Share evaluation results with third parties
(c) Reverse engineer any prototype hardware or software
(d) Use Confidential Information to develop competing products
(e) File any patent applications based on Discloser's Confidential Information

---

## 4. Prototype and Sample Handling

### 4.1 Prototype Custody

All prototype hardware, evaluation boards, and physical samples remain the property of Discloser at all times. Recipient is granted temporary custody solely for the Evaluation Purpose.

### 4.2 Prototype Security Requirements

| Requirement | Detail |
|-------------|--------|
| Physical security | Prototypes stored in locked lab with access control |
| Inventory tracking | Each unit tracked by serial number; location logged |
| Access limitation | Only named evaluation team members may access |
| Photography | No external photography of prototypes without written consent |
| Disassembly | No disassembly, X-ray, or destructive analysis without written consent |
| Network isolation | Prototypes not connected to internet-accessible networks |

### 4.3 Return or Destruction Obligation

Upon the earlier of: (a) completion of evaluation, (b) expiration of evaluation period, or (c) written request by Discloser:

- Recipient shall return all prototypes within [10 business days]
- Alternatively, upon Discloser's written instruction, Recipient shall destroy prototypes and provide witnessed destruction certificate
- Recipient shall delete all digital copies of evaluation data derived from prototypes
- Recipient shall certify in writing that all materials have been returned or destroyed

---

## 5. Enhanced Trade Secret Protection

For information designated "TRADE SECRET" or "RESTRICTED":

(a) Access limited to a maximum of [5] named individuals at Recipient organization
(b) Named individuals must be disclosed to Discloser in writing before access
(c) No copies permitted (paper or electronic) without Discloser's written consent
(d) Access only in Discloser-approved secure facility
(e) Recipient shall implement additional access controls as reasonably requested by Discloser

---

## 6. Intellectual Property Rights

### 6.1 Ownership

All Confidential Information and intellectual property contained therein remains the sole property of Discloser. No license, express or implied, is granted by this Agreement except the limited right to evaluate as described herein.

### 6.2 No Implied License

Disclosure of Confidential Information does not grant Recipient any rights under any patents, copyrights, trade secrets, or other intellectual property of Discloser.

### 6.3 Feedback

If Recipient provides feedback, suggestions, or improvement ideas regarding the Confidential Information, Discloser shall own all intellectual property rights in such feedback without obligation to Recipient.

---

## 7. Non-Solicitation

During the term of this Agreement and for [12 months] following termination, Recipient shall not directly solicit for employment any employee of Discloser who was involved in the disclosure of Confidential Information under this Agreement, without prior written consent.

---

## 8. Remedies

### 8.1 Injunctive Relief

Recipient acknowledges that unauthorized disclosure or use of Confidential Information would cause irreparable harm. Discloser shall be entitled to seek injunctive relief without posting bond.

### 8.2 Accountability

Recipient is responsible for any breach by its employees, contractors, or agents who received Confidential Information. Recipient shall indemnify Discloser for all damages resulting from breach.

---

## 9. Term and Termination

(a) **Evaluation Period**: [6/12 months] from Effective Date (renewable by mutual agreement)
(b) **Termination for Convenience**: Either Party may terminate upon [30 days] written notice
(c) **Termination for Breach**: Immediate termination upon material breach
(d) **Survival**: Sections 2, 4.3, 5, 6, 7, and 8 survive termination for 5 years after last disclosure

---

## 10. General Provisions

(a) **Governing Law**: [Jurisdiction] law governs without conflict of laws principles
(b) **Dispute Resolution**: [Arbitration/Litigation] in [venue]
(c) **Entire Agreement**: This Agreement is the complete agreement on confidentiality
(d) **Amendment**: Written amendments signed by both Parties required
(e) **No Obligation to Transact**: This Agreement does not obligate either Party to enter into any further business relationship
(f) **Export Control**: Recipient shall comply with all applicable export control laws
