## MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

**This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement ("Agreement")** is entered into as of [Date] ("Effective Date") by and between:

**[COMPANY NAME]** ("Company"), a [jurisdiction] corporation with principal offices at [address]

and

**[VENDOR NAME]** ("Vendor"), a [jurisdiction] entity with principal offices at [address]

(each a "Party" and collectively the "Parties")

---

## 1. Purpose

The Parties wish to explore and/or engage in a business relationship related to [describe general scope, e.g., "development of automotive camera module hardware"] (the "Purpose"). In connection with the Purpose, each Party may disclose to the other certain confidential and proprietary information.

---

## 2. Definition of Confidential Information

"Confidential Information" means all non-public information disclosed by either Party to the other, whether orally, in writing, or by inspection of tangible objects, that is designated as "Confidential," "Proprietary," "Restricted," or "Trade Secret," or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential given the nature of the information and circumstances of disclosure. Confidential Information includes, without limitation:

(a) Technical information: designs, drawings, specifications, schematics, PCB layouts, firmware source code, algorithms, pinout configurations, signal routing topologies, thermal management designs, calibration parameters, test procedures, manufacturing processes

(b) Business information: pricing, cost structures, customer lists, supplier terms, business plans, market analyses, financial projections

(c) Intellectual property: invention disclosures, patent applications (before publication), trade secrets as cataloged by the disclosing Party

(d) Samples and prototypes: any physical hardware, prototype boards, or evaluation units provided

---

## 3. Exclusions

Confidential Information does not include information that:

(a) Is or becomes publicly available through no fault of the receiving Party;

(b) Was rightfully in the receiving Party's possession before disclosure, as evidenced by written records;

(c) Is rightfully obtained by the receiving Party from a third party without restriction on disclosure;

(d) Is independently developed by the receiving Party without use of or reference to the disclosing Party's Confidential Information, as evidenced by written records;

(e) Is required to be disclosed by law, regulation, or court order, provided that the receiving Party gives the disclosing Party prompt written notice and cooperates in seeking a protective order.

---

## 4. Obligations of Receiving Party

The receiving Party shall:

(a) Hold all Confidential Information in strict confidence using at least the same degree of care it uses to protect its own confidential information, but in no event less than reasonable care;

(b) Not disclose Confidential Information to any third party without prior written consent of the disclosing Party;

(c) Limit internal disclosure to employees, contractors, and agents who (i) have a need to know for the Purpose, and (ii) are bound by confidentiality obligations at least as protective as this Agreement;

(d) Not use Confidential Information for any purpose other than the Purpose;

(e) Not reverse engineer, disassemble, or decompile any prototypes, samples, or hardware provided as Confidential Information;

(f) Maintain a log of all personnel who receive access to Confidential Information;

(g) Promptly notify the disclosing Party of any unauthorized disclosure or use of Confidential Information.

---

## 5. Intellectual Property Assignment

(a) All intellectual property created by Vendor in the course of performing work for Company using Company's Confidential Information or within the scope of work defined in any associated Statement of Work shall be the sole and exclusive property of Company.

(b) Vendor hereby assigns and agrees to assign to Company all right, title, and interest in and to such intellectual property, including all patent rights, copyrights, trade secret rights, and other intellectual property rights.

(c) Vendor shall execute all documents and take all actions reasonably necessary to perfect Company's ownership of such intellectual property.

(d) Vendor retains ownership of its pre-existing intellectual property. Any pre-existing IP incorporated into deliverables must be disclosed in writing and licensed to Company on a non-exclusive, perpetual, royalty-free basis.

---

## 6. Non-Compete for Platform Designs

(a) During the term of this Agreement and for [2 years] following termination, Vendor shall not:

- (i) Develop, manufacture, or sell products that are substantially similar to Company's platform designs disclosed as Confidential Information;
- (ii) Use Company's Confidential Information to assist any competitor of Company in developing competing products;
- (iii) Solicit or accept engagements from Company's identified competitors [list competitors if known] for work in the same technology domain as the Purpose.

(b) This non-compete applies solely to the specific technology domain of [automotive dual-camera vision modules / adaptive SoC carrier platforms] and does not restrict Vendor's general business activities in unrelated fields.

(c) The Parties acknowledge that this restriction is reasonable in scope, duration, and geographic extent given the nature of the Confidential Information disclosed.

---

## 7. Audit Rights

(a) Company shall have the right, upon [30 days] written notice, to audit Vendor's compliance with this Agreement, including inspection of:

- (i) Access control systems and logs for Confidential Information;
- (ii) Physical security measures for prototypes and hardware samples;
- (iii) Personnel records confirming individual confidentiality agreements;
- (iv) Data handling and destruction procedures.

(b) Audits shall be conducted during normal business hours and shall not unreasonably interfere with Vendor's operations.

(c) Company shall bear the cost of audits unless a material breach is discovered, in which case Vendor shall bear the cost.

(d) Company may conduct audits no more than [once per year] during the term, except in the event of a suspected breach, in which case additional audits are permitted.

---

## 8. Breach and Remedies

(a) **Injunctive Relief**: The Parties acknowledge that any breach of this Agreement may cause irreparable harm for which monetary damages are an inadequate remedy. Accordingly, the disclosing Party shall be entitled to seek injunctive or other equitable relief without the necessity of posting a bond.

(b) **Liquidated Damages**: In addition to equitable relief, the breaching Party agrees to pay liquidated damages of [amount] per incident of unauthorized disclosure, acknowledging that actual damages from trade secret misappropriation are difficult to calculate.

(c) **Indemnification**: The breaching Party shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless the non-breaching Party from all losses, damages, costs, and expenses (including reasonable attorney's fees) arising from the breach.

(d) **Notification**: Upon discovery of any actual or suspected breach, the receiving Party shall notify the disclosing Party within [24 hours] in writing.

---

## 9. Return and Destruction

(a) Upon termination of this Agreement or upon written request by the disclosing Party:

- (i) The receiving Party shall promptly return all tangible Confidential Information, including all copies, extracts, and derivatives;
- (ii) The receiving Party shall permanently delete all electronic copies of Confidential Information from all systems;
- (iii) The receiving Party shall physically destroy all prototypes and hardware samples (with certification of destruction);
- (iv) The receiving Party shall certify in writing, signed by an officer, that all Confidential Information has been returned or destroyed.

(b) Notwithstanding the foregoing, the receiving Party may retain one (1) archival copy solely for purposes of monitoring compliance with this Agreement, provided such copy is stored securely with restricted access.

---

## 10. Term and Termination

(a) This Agreement is effective as of the Effective Date and continues for [5 years] unless terminated earlier.

(b) Either Party may terminate this Agreement upon [30 days] written notice.

(c) The obligations of confidentiality, non-use, IP assignment, and non-compete survive termination for [5 years] from the date of last disclosure of Confidential Information.

---

## 11. General Provisions

(a) **Governing Law**: This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of [jurisdiction] without regard to conflict of laws provisions.

(b) **Dispute Resolution**: Any dispute arising under this Agreement shall be resolved by [arbitration/litigation] in [venue].

(c) **Entire Agreement**: This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties regarding confidentiality and supersedes all prior agreements on the subject.

(d) **Amendment**: This Agreement may only be modified by written instrument signed by both Parties.

(e) **Assignment**: Neither Party may assign this Agreement without prior written consent of the other Party.

(f) **Severability**: If any provision is held unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force and effect.

(g) **No License**: Nothing in this Agreement grants either Party any license or right to use the other Party's intellectual property except as expressly stated herein.
