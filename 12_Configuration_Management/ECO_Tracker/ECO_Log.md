## Usage Instructions

When an ECO is initiated, add a row to the register above with:

1. **ECO #**: Sequential number (ECO-2026-001, ECO-2026-002, etc.)
2. **Date**: Date of initiation (YYYY-MM-DD)
3. **Originator**: Person requesting the change
4. **Title**: Brief descriptive title of the change
5. **Affected Docs**: List of documents/files impacted
6. **Impact Level**: Minor / Moderate / Major / Critical
7. **Cost Impact**: Per-unit cost change estimate
8. **Schedule Impact**: Days/weeks of delay (if any)
9. **Status**: Current status (see ECO_Process.md for definitions)
10. **Approval Date**: Date approval was granted

## Metrics

Track the following metrics quarterly:
- ECO volume (total initiated per quarter)
- Average turnaround time (initiation to close)
- Rejection rate
- Impact distribution (Minor/Moderate/Major/Critical)
