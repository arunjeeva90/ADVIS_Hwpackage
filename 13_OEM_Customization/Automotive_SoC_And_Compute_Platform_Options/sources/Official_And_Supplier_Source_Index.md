## Pricing cautions

Public SoC prices are often low-volume distributor prices and do not represent automotive programme pricing. RFQ prices may exclude:

- PMIC/memory
- safety BSP and MCAL
- compiler/tool licence
- camera and ISP tuning
- QNX/RTOS licence
- NRE
- PPAP
- support
- customs, GST and freight

Marketplace and board prices are sourcing observations, not approved supplier status.
