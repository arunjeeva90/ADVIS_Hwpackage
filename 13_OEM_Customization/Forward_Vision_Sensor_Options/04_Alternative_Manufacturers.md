# Alternative and Less-Utilized Manufacturers

Sony, OmniVision and onsemi dominate many automotive camera evaluations, but ADVIS should keep a broader supplier funnel.

The following official-product alternatives were added:

- Samsung ISOCELL Auto
- SmartSens Automotive AT Series
- STMicroelectronics SafeSense / automotive CMOS sensors
- Pixelplus automotive sensors
- Brigates
- PixArt PrimeSensor

Inclusion is not production approval. Automotive sensing, automotive viewing, dashcam and research products must be evaluated separately.
